## Does the AGPL affect you?

It depends on one question: **does what you build reach anyone else?**

| your situation | AGPL is enough |
|---|---|
| research, study, evaluation, internal prototype | yes |
| internal tool that never leaves your organisation and is not offered over a network | usually yes |
| software you distribute to customers | no |
| SaaS or any service users reach over a network | no — this is the clause that distinguishes AGPL from GPL |
| firmware or a device you sell | no |
| embedding in a proprietary database, engine or platform | no |

The line is not a trap and it is not an accident. The AGPL is what makes an
open, auditable, citable version possible without giving away the commercial
right — and it is also what starts the conversation on this page.

If you are unsure which side of the line you are on, ask. It is a short
conversation and it costs nothing.

## What the commercial licence includes

- The right to build Smart2Raw into software you do not publish under AGPL.
- Access to the **advanced version**, which is not published.
- Support and priority on corrections.
- Terms adapted to how you deploy — per product, per device, or per site.
