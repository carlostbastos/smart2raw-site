# Privacy

Short version: **the demonstration sends nothing, and the contact form is the
only place on this site that receives information from you.**

## The demonstration

The library on the home page is compiled to WebAssembly and runs inside your
browser. The column you paste, drop or generate is processed in your own device's
memory. It is not uploaded, not logged and not seen by anyone — there is no
server on the other side to send it to.

That is why you can test with data you would not normally share. If you want to
verify the claim rather than trust it, open your browser's network panel while
you use it, or download the single-file version and run it with the network
disconnected.

## The contact form

If you write to us, we receive what you type: name, e-mail, and whatever you put
in the message and the qualifying fields. It is used to reply to you and to
evaluate a possible commercial licence — nothing else. It is not sold, not
shared, and not used to build a marketing list.

The form is delivered by a third-party form service, which handles the message in
transit, and it arrives in an e-mail inbox. Ask and we will delete your message
and your details; ask and we will tell you what we hold.

## Cookies and measurement

This site sets no cookies and runs no advertising or cross-site tracking.

## Hosting

The site is served as static files by a hosting provider, which — like every web
server — records ordinary request logs including IP addresses, for security and
operation. We do not combine those logs with anything else.
